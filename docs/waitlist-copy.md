AWAITING HUMAN DECISION in BUILD-LOG).

## Concept A — the coach angle

**Headline:** Find what slows your typing and fix it in 15 minutes a day.

**Subhead:** RealType shows you exactly which keys, pairs, and habits cost you
speed, then gives you short drills that target them.

## Concept B — the programmer angle

**Headline:** Symbols, numbers, and naming: typing practice built for
programmers.

**Subhead:** Brackets, operators, strings, numbers, identifiers — RealType's
programmer track drills the keys and patterns that actually appear in code.

## Concept C — the own-your-code angle

**Headline:** We're exploring typing practice you can run on your own code,
entirely on your device. Would you use it?

**Subhead:** Nothing to install: paste or pick code, practice on it, and keep
everything on your machine. Tell us if this is worth building.

## Standing facts (shown on every variant)

- RealType is in development — this page is for the waitlist, not the product
  itself.
- RealType is for adults 18 and over.
- We make no promises about speed gains, programming ability, or hiring
  outcomes — only about what you practice.
